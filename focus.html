Focus areas

The decisions that shape delivery.

Four areas where I have spent most of my working time — from writing requirements before a contract is signed to testing equipment before it is energized.

01 / Requirements & review 02 / HV package engineering 03 / Commissioning & energization 04 / Interface coordination

Focus 01

Requirements & technical review

Making requirements clear before commitments are made.

Owner’s-side engineering for HV designs, equipment specifications and supplier offers.

  • Employer’s Requirements and technical specifications
  • Tender clarification and technical bid evaluation
  • Design review and compliance assessment

Typical deliverables: a requirements document, technical evaluation or consolidated design review.

Example: Formosa 4 →

Focus 02

HV package engineering

Keeping equipment decisions connected to delivery.

Coordination across equipment engineering, procurement, vendor documentation and fabrication interfaces.

  • Transformers, shunt reactors, EATs and GIS
  • HV / MV cables, busducts and package interfaces
  • Technical variations, vendor reviews and FAT coordination

Typical deliverables: a package review, technical action register or equipment interface review.

Example: Fengmiao I →

Focus 03

Commissioning & energization

Building a clear path from testing to readiness.

Technical preparation and field work for equipment verification, integrated testing and energization planning.

  • FAT / SAT / HAT / SIT procedure preparation
  • Protection testing and commissioning
  • Test sequencing and technical readiness review

Typical deliverables: a test procedure, commissioning plan or documented technical findings.

Example: Greater Changhua 1 & 2a →

Focus 04

Interface coordination

Closing the gaps between organisations and systems.

Work at the boundaries where owners, engineering teams, EPC contractors, OEMs and fabrication yards need coordinated technical decisions.

  • Electrical, spatial and equipment interface coordination
  • Technical issue resolution across project partners
  • Engineering-to-yard and grid-connection interfaces

Typical deliverables: an interface register, coordinated action list or technical recommendation.

Example: East Anglia ONE North →

How I work

Start with the scope.

The same three habits carry across every project stage.

  1. 01

    Define

    Clarify the interfaces, the available information and what the deliverable needs to be.

  2. 02

    Align

    Agree responsibilities and sequence with the teams on each side of the interface.

  3. 03

    Close out

    Work through the technical questions to clear findings, actions and handover.
