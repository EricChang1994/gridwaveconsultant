Public project context

Project overview

East Anglia ONE North is an offshore wind development in the southern North Sea off the Suffolk coast, developed by East Anglia ONE North Ltd within ScottishPower Renewables’ East Anglia portfolio. It forms part of the East Anglia Hub and received development consent in March 2022. The founder’s work focuses on the offshore substation’s HV, protection and selected mechanical-system electrical interfaces.
