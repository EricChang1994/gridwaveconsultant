PUBLIC PROJECT CONTEXT

Project overview

Mai-Liao Power Corporation was established in 1996 by companies within the Formosa group. Its original independent power project comprised three 600 MW generating units, commissioned between 1999 and 2000, at the Mailiao industrial park in Yunlin County. This profile focuses on the founder’s 2020–2021 high-voltage operations and maintenance experience.
