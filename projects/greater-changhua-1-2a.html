PUBLIC PROJECT CONTEXT

Project overview

Greater Changhua 1 and 2a is a 900 MW offshore wind development in Taiwan. Ørsted inaugurated the wind farms in April 2024 and reported that they were operating and fully connected to the grid. The developer’s milestone record notes onshore substation energization in February 2022 and first wind farm power in April 2022.
