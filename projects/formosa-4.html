Public project context

Project overview

Formosa 4 is a 495 MW offshore wind project developed by Synera Renewable Energy off Miaoli County. The project website describes a site of approximately 58 km², with its nearest point around 20 km from shore. Its planned electrical infrastructure includes 500 MW offshore and onshore substations, with an intended connection to Taipower’s Fangli substation.
