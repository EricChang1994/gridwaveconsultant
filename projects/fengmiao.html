Public project context

Project overview

Fengmiao I is a 495 MW offshore wind development off Taiwan’s west coast. Developed through a Copenhagen Infrastructure Partners fund, with Copenhagen Offshore Partners leading development, it reached financial close in March 2025. The planned wind farm comprises 33 Vestas 15 MW turbines, with construction completion targeted for the end of 2027 in the developer’s announcement.

Engineering perspective

What I took from it

An offshore substation brings many separately procured packages into one electrical system. Equipment interfaces, protection requirements and test sequencing need to remain aligned as the design moves into installation and commissioning.
