PUBLIC PROJECT CONTEXT

Project overview

Greater Changhua 2b and 4 is Ørsted’s 920 MW offshore wind development, located 35–60 km off Taiwan’s Changhua coast. The project comprises 66 Siemens Gamesa 14 MW turbines. Ørsted reported completion of turbine installation in January 2026 and held a ceremony marking completion of major construction works in September 2026, with final commissioning activities still described in that announcement.
